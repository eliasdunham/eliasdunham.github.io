Focus

The through-line in my work is instrumentation under conditions that break ordinary measurement: pressure, temperature, tight volume budgets. I am most interested in extending that toward experimental physics and aerospace, where the hard part is usually building the thing that lets you observe the system at all.

I am currently looking for a co-op or research position where I can keep designing test hardware and characterizing materials.
